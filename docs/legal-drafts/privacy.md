# Privacy Policy

Studiskarte Marketplace ("the app", "we") is a marketplace where students and other members of the school community in the Philippines offer things and services and look for things they want. This policy says what personal data the app collects, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it. Last updated [date of adoption].

## At a glance

- **Who we are.** The app is run by one person, [Owner's full name], not a company. Write to us at support@studiskarte.com. Our Data Protection Officer is [DPO name and dedicated email to be designated].
- **16 and over.** You must be 16 or older to use the app. Members under 18 should tell a parent or guardian. We ask you to confirm your age when you sign up.
- **What we collect.** Your email address, a scrambled form of your password, your handle and display name, and what you post and send in the app: listings, photos, messages, offers, reservations and reviews. We also keep basic sign-in details (your network address and browser) for 30 days.
- **ID check (optional, later).** When it opens, you may choose to show a school ID or certificate of registration and a photo of you holding it. We ask for your consent on a separate screen. Only the owner sees the pictures, and they are deleted as soon as we decide.
- **No ads, no selling.** We do not use advertising or analytics trackers. We do not sell your data. We never handle money: payments happen between members, outside the app.
- **Who else handles your data.** Vercel, Neon, Cloudflare, Resend and Sentry help us run the app, and Google and push services only if you choose them. Most of them are outside the Philippines. Details are below.
- **Deleting your account.** You can delete your account in Settings. Some things stay without your name, for example messages and reviews other members rely on. Details are below.
- **Your rights.** You can ask to see, correct or erase your data, object to its use, get a copy, or withdraw your consent. Write to us. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the National Privacy Commission.

## What we collect

When you create an account:

- **Your email address.** A school email address (ending in .edu.ph) or another email address. We send a 6-digit code to it to prove it is yours.
- **Your password,** kept only as a salted hash. We cannot read it.
- **If you add a school email later,** that school email, the school it belongs to, and the role you choose (student, faculty or teacher, staff, alumni). Your school and role are information about your education. We use them only to show your school and role and to decide what you may do in the app (for example, only students can post a Showcase).
- **If you use Google to sign in,** Google sends us your basic profile (the email address it confirms for you, your name and your picture). We keep only the email address and discard the name and picture. We do not ask for or read anything else in your Google account.
- **Your handle and display name,** which other signed-in members see, and an optional profile picture.
- **That you are 16 or older,** which version of the Terms and this policy you accepted, and when.

When you use the app:

- **Listings:** the title, description, price, category, photos, availability and where you meet. Photos have their location and camera data removed when they are uploaded.
- **Messages and pictures** you send to another member, offers, reservations, handover times and places, problem reports about a reservation, and the reviews you give and receive.
- **Activity:** who you follow, listings you heart (hearts are private), people you block, reports you make about listings or people, and views of listings (counted once per person per day and shown only to the listing's owner as a number).
- **Device and session data:** when you sign in we store a session with your network (IP) address and browser details, for 30 days or until you sign out. Sign-in attempt counters can include your email address or network address and are kept for about a day.
- **Device alerts,** if you switch them on: an address your phone's or browser's push service gives us. Alerts show a headline and a listing title, never message text.
- **Calendar link,** if you make one: only a fingerprint (hash) of the private link is stored.
- **Emails you send us.** If you write to support@studiskarte.com we keep the email and our reply.

How we collect it: from you, when you type, upload or tap; from Google, if you choose Google sign-in; and from your browser, which sends a network address and browser details with every request.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers. The only cookie is the one that keeps you signed in.

## Why we use it, and our legal reason

- **To create your account, sign you in and keep it safe** (codes, passwords, sign-in limits). Reason: it is needed to give you the service you asked for.
- **To run the marketplace:** show listings, let members message each other, make offers and reservations, arrange a handover, and show reviews. Reason: the same.
- **To send you the emails the app needs.** The app sends email only for sign-in and sign-up codes, password reset codes and school email codes. Notices about your listings and reservations appear inside the app and, if you switch alerts on, as device alerts.
- **To keep the community safe:** reports, blocks, limits on spam, and review by the owner of reported listings and people. Reason: our legitimate interest, and other members' interest, in a safe marketplace, balanced against your rights.
- **To find and fix errors** (error reports, see Sentry below). Reason: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working.
- **Your school and role.** We rely on your agreement, given when you sign up and accept the Terms and this policy.
- **ID verification,** when it opens: only with your separate consent. See the section below.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising. We never handle payments: money changes hands between members outside the app.

## What others can see

Signed-in members can see your handle, display name, picture, school (shown as the school name, or "Visitor" if you have none), the number of followers you have, your listings, your reviews and ratings, and how many items you sold or gave away. Your email address, school email and password are never shown to other members. Messages are visible only to the two people in the conversation (and to the owner if one of you reports it).

## Who else handles your data

We use these services to run the app. Each receives only what it needs for its job. We stay responsible for your data while they handle it for us.

- **Vercel** hosts the app. It sees every request to the app, including network addresses and browser details, in its logs for a short time. Location: servers outside the Philippines [Vercel function region: confirm].
- **Neon** hosts the database, in the United States. Everything you type into the app is stored there.
- **Cloudflare** does three things for us. It runs the domain name service for studiskarte.com. It passes mail sent to support@studiskarte.com on to the owner's own mailbox [mailbox provider: owner to name]. And its storage service, **R2**, holds listing photos, message pictures and profile pictures, and (when it opens) ID verification images in a separate private area. Pictures can only be reached through short-lived links that the app makes for signed-in members, and ID images only for the owner. Location: [R2 bucket location: confirm].
- **Resend** sends our emails. It sees the address we send to and the message, which is the code. Location: [Resend region: confirm].
- **Sentry** receives error reports from the app and from your browser when something breaks. We strip personal data before an error report leaves the app: no user identity, cookies, request headers, request contents or anything after a "?" in a web address, and email addresses are blanked in the text. A report can still show technical details such as the page, the kind of browser and the error. Sentry's servers are outside the Philippines [Sentry data region: confirm from the Sentry project settings].
- **Google** is involved only if you choose to sign in with Google. Google sees that you are signing in to Studiskarte.
- **Push services** run by your phone's or browser's maker (for example Apple, Google or Mozilla) carry device alerts, only if you switch them on.

- **What we collect.** A picture of your school ID or certificate of registration, and a photo of you holding it. These show information about your education and are issued by your school, so we treat them with extra care.
- **Why.** Only to decide whether to give your account an "ID checked" badge for the school and role you chose [state here whether the check also confirms that you meet the minimum age: owner to decide before launch]. We do not use the pictures for anything else.

- **Who sees it.** Only the owner. The pictures are kept in a private area, separate from listing photos, and the owner opens them only through short-lived links. Each time the pictures are opened is logged. We do not share them with anyone, and they do not appear anywhere in the app.

## How long we keep it

- Your account data: until you delete your account.
- Photos you uploaded but never used in a listing or message: deleted after 24 hours.
- Listings you withdraw or that expire disappear from your lists after a day (withdrawn) or a week (expired), but they are kept in our database together with their photos, offers and reservations, so the history and reviews of other members stay correct.

## Deleting your account

In **Settings, Delete my account** you can delete your account at any time. This erases your email and school email (the closed account keeps a random address no one can sign in with), handle, display name, picture, password, Google link, sessions, device alerts, notifications, follows, hearts, blocks, view history and calendar link. It withdraws your open listings, closes your pending offers, and ends reservations still being arranged. The other person is told.

These stay, without your name: your messages and message pictures, finished reservations, reviews, and your listings' text and photos that other members' history depends on. Reports you made stay too. If you want any of that removed as well, write to us and we will look at each item [lawyer: confirm which items must be erased on request and which may stay]. An account that is an administrator account cannot be deleted in Settings; write to us.

Deleting is final. You can sign up again with the same address, but as a new member.

## Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) you have the right to:

- **Be informed** about how your data is used. This policy is part of that.
- **Access** your data on request: what we hold, where it came from, who received it, how it was used and when it was last accessed or changed.
- **Correct** it if it is wrong.
- **Erase or block** it, for example if it is no longer needed, was unlawfully obtained or is used in a way you did not agree to.
- **Object** to how we use it.
- **Get a copy** in a common electronic format (data portability).
- **Withdraw your consent** at any time, where we rely on it. Withdrawing does not affect what we did before.
- **Claim compensation** for damage caused by wrongful use of your data.
- If you have died or cannot act, your **lawful heirs or assigns** may use these rights for you.

**How to use them.** Email support@studiskarte.com, or our Data Protection Officer at [DPO name and dedicated email to be designated]. Write from the email address on your account, and say which right you want to use. Some things you can do yourself: change your picture, display name and handle, switch device alerts off, remove your calendar link, block someone, and delete your account, all in Settings. We may ask you to confirm who you are before we give out or change anything, and we will ask for no more than we need.

**How fast.** We will answer within [X days, lawyer to set]. If we cannot do what you ask, or can do only part of it, we will say why.

**Complaints.** If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph). The Commission's rules generally expect you to tell us in writing first and to give us 15 calendar days to respond, unless the Commission excuses that [lawyer: confirm].

You do not give up any of these rights by accepting the Terms or this policy.

## If something goes wrong

If a personal data breach puts you at real risk of serious harm, we will tell you and the National Privacy Commission within 72 hours of learning about it, as the Commission's rules require [lawyer: confirm]. We will tell you by email, individually, say what happened, what data was involved, what we have done and who to contact, and say how you can protect yourself. ## How we protect your data

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Pictures can only be reached through short-lived links. Error reports are stripped of personal data before they leave the app. Administrator rights are checked in the database on every action. These measures reduce risk but cannot remove it.
