# Refund, Returns and Disputes Policy

This policy is for deals between members of Studiskarte Marketplace ("the app"). Last updated [date of adoption]. It sits beside the Terms of Service, the Privacy Policy and the Community Guidelines.

## At a glance

- The app never handles money. We cannot hold a payment, give one back, or force anyone to refund.
- **Look at the item and test it before you pay.** Say what you will check in the chat first.
- Sales between members are generally final once the handover is done and the payment is confirmed. There are exceptions, below.
- You can cancel a reservation until payment is under way, cash in person is agreed, or one of you says the handover happened. After that, use **Report what happened**.
- Paying a member for a service has no protection from the app.
- If you are under 18, tell a parent or guardian, and do not meet alone.
- Nothing here takes away a right you have under Philippine law.

## How a deal works in the app

1. A member makes an offer or reserves a listing. An offer that gets no answer expires after 3 days.
2. When the offer is accepted, the units are held for the buyer. This is a reservation.
3. The two of you agree a time and place. A meet-up must be between 6:00 AM and 8:00 PM, Philippine time. A handover can be planned up to 60 days ahead. If no time and place are agreed within 7 days, the reservation is cancelled.
4. If the listing has a price, you settle payment as the listing says (see "Payment" below).
5. Each of you taps to say the handover happened. When both have done so and the payment is confirmed, the deal is **Completed**.
6. Each of you can then leave the other a review of 1 to 5 stars and an optional note. A review is never edited. Other members can see it.

If a reservation is scheduled and neither of you confirms within 7 days after the agreed time, it is closed. It is cancelled if it was never secured, and marked as reported if it was secured.

## Payment: what the app does and does not do

- **The app never moves money.** A listing says whether it takes **Cash**, **Online payment**, or **Others**, and may have a short note (for example where to pay online). You pay each other directly, outside the app.
- The app only keeps a record of what you each say. It does not check that money was paid or received.
- The steps are two-way. For online payment or others: the one being paid asks the other to pay, the payer taps "I've paid", and the one being paid taps "Payment received", or "Not received" if nothing arrived. For cash: the payer asks to pay cash in person, the one being paid agrees, and at the handover the payer taps "I gave the cash" and the one being paid taps "I received the cash".
- **Confirm only if you really have the money.** Tapping "Payment received" or "I received the cash" is your statement to the other member and to us.
- When the payer says they paid, or cash in person is agreed, the slot is **secured** and the reservation can no longer be cancelled.
- A free listing has nothing to pay.

## Before you pay: inspect and test

Most problems can be avoided at the meeting. Before you pay or confirm:

- **Textbooks and notes:** check that pages and chapters are complete and readable.
- **Gadgets and electronics:** switch them on, try the buttons, screen and charger.
- **Clothes and uniforms:** check the size, zips, buttons and any stains against the listing.
- **Anything else:** compare it with the photos and the description, including the condition the seller chose.

You may say no and leave if the item is not as described. You do not have to buy it. Do not confirm the handover or the payment if you are not satisfied.

## When a sale is final

When the handover is done and the payment is confirmed, a sale between members is generally **final**. The seller is an ordinary student, not a shop. We do not offer a "change of mind" refund. [lawyer: confirm this statement is acceptable, see the notes.]

## When you may still ask for a refund

- **An agreed testing period.** For items that are costly or hard to check on the spot (a laptop, a calculator), you and the seller can agree in the chat, before you pay, on a testing period (for example 24 to 48 hours). If the seller agreed, they should refund you if the item fails in that time. The chat is your record. Screenshots help.
- **Misrepresentation.** If the seller said something untrue about the item, or hid a serious fault they knew about (for example, an item sold as "working" that never worked), you may ask for your money back and may report it.
- **Hidden defects under the Civil Code.** The Civil Code says the seller answers for hidden defects that make the thing unfit for its use, or lower its fitness so much that the buyer would not have bought it, or would have paid less (Art. 1561). The seller is not answerable for defects you could see, or, if you are an expert in that thing by trade or profession, for those you should have known (Art. 1561). The seller is answerable even if they did not know of the defect (Art. 1566). That does not apply if the contrary was agreed and the seller did not know of the defect (Art. 1566). You may withdraw from the sale or ask for a lower price, with damages in either case (Art. 1567). This kind of claim is barred after six months from delivery (Art. 1571). Any statement of fact or promise by the seller that made you buy can be an express warranty (Art. 1546). [lawyer: confirm how these articles apply to a used item sold between students and to an "as is" statement in a listing.]
- **No-show or no delivery.** If the other member did not show up or deliver, you can report it (see "If something goes wrong"). If you had already paid, ask them to return the money.

Where a seller agrees to a refund, it is paid the same way as the original payment, directly between you. The buyer should return the item in the condition received.

## Cancelling and not showing up

- Either of you can cancel a reservation while it is still being arranged. The units go back on the listing and the other member is told. This cannot be undone.
- Once payment is under way, cash in person is agreed, or one of you said the handover happened, it **cannot be cancelled** any more. Report what happened instead.
- Please cancel in good time if plans change. The Terms ask you to show up to meetings you agree to.
- A member who often does not show up, or stops replying, can be reported. See "What happens to members who break the rules".

## Services paid outside the app

Services (tutoring, printing, design and the like) carry more risk than a thing you can inspect first.

- The app never holds the money for a service. **There is no protection from the app if you pay in advance and the service is not done.**
- Agree in the chat what will be done, by when, and the price, before anyone pays.
- Where you can, pay in parts or after each part is done, and do not pay everything first to someone you do not know.
- If the service was not done, or was done badly, ask for a refund of the part not done, and report it if the other member does not respond.
- A service meant to help someone cheat or break their school's rules is not allowed, and we will not help recover money paid for one.

A minor who must give back what they got is only obliged to return what they benefited from (Art. 1399). We do not decide whether a particular deal is valid. ## If something goes wrong

1. **Talk first.** Message the other member in the chat. Most problems are misunderstandings.
2. **Cancel or report on the reservation.**
   - Before payment is under way, **cancel** the reservation.
   - Once it cannot be cancelled, or the agreed time has passed, use **Report what happened**. Say whose end the problem was (yours, theirs, or not sure), pick a reason (did not show up or deliver; not as described; a problem with the payment; stopped replying; something else) and write what happened (up to 500 characters).
   - Reporting **closes the reservation**. The units go back on the listing, nothing is counted as a handover, and the other member is told. Each of you can add your side once. You both see what the other wrote.
   - A report on a reservation records what each of you says. [It does not by itself go to the owner for a decision: see the notes.]
3. **Report the listing or the person.** To ask the owner to look at a listing or a member, use **Report** on the listing or the person, or email support@studiskarte.com. Give the link to the listing or the member's handle, what happened, and screenshots of the listing and the chat. See the Terms, "Reporting a problem and taking listings down", for what happens next.
4. **Complaints about our decision.** Email support@studiskarte.com. See the Terms, "Complaints and disagreement".
5. **Outside the app.** You may go to the barangay (for a settlement between you), the Department of Trade and Industry, the police for suspected fraud, or the courts. [lawyer: confirm which routes to name, see the notes.]

## What we cannot do

- **We cannot hold, collect, refund or send money.** The app never has it.
- We cannot make a member refund you or return an item.
- We cannot decide who is legally right. We are not a court, and we are not a party to the deal.
- We cannot check that an item is as described, that a service was done, or that a payment was made. The app only records what members say.
- We cannot promise a result or a time for the review of a deal. The owner is one person. We will say so if we need more time.
- We cannot give legal advice.

## What happens to members who break the rules

The owner may hide or remove a listing, or suspend an account, if a member scams, misleads, harasses, or keeps breaking the rules. We do not suspend accounts automatically. A listing reported by three different members is hidden at once and goes to the owner for review. Reviews and reports may be seen by the owner when deciding. ## Your rights under the law

This policy does not remove or limit any right you have under Philippine law.

- The Civil Code rules on hidden defects (above) apply to sales whether or not this policy mentions them.
- The Consumer Act (RA 7394) and the Internet Transactions Act (RA 11967) may or may not apply to a deal between two members. The Internet Transactions Act says it does not cover consumer-to-consumer transactions (Sec. 3), which it defines as transactions between end-users for personal, family or household purposes and not in the ordinary course of business (Sec. 4(c)). A member who sells regularly as a business may fall outside that exception. [lawyer: confirm.] Where the Internet Transactions Act applies, it says an online consumer may pursue repair, replacement, refund or other remedies under RA 7394 or other relevant laws (Sec. 20), and that the Civil Code rules on sales apply (Sec. 33).
- The Consumer Act protects "consumers" who buy consumer products and services, and describes deceptive acts by a seller or supplier (Art. 50) and liability for product imperfections and defective services (Arts. 99, 100). Whether an occasional student seller is a "seller" or "supplier" under it is not settled here. [to confirm]
