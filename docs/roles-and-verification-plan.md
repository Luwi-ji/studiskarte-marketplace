Status: **partly built.** Built 2026-10-19 (ADR 0005): roles in the database (student, faculty, staff, alumni, visitor), password login, visitor sign-up behind `ALLOW_VISITORS`, adding a school email later from Settings (code to the school address, then role), Google sign-in. Built 2026-10-20 (migration 0029): only students can post a showcase (the others post wishlists); each showcase has "allow non-students" (`post.allow_non_students`, default off, so existing listings stay students-only); a non-student can start a conversation, make an offer or reserve only on a showcase that allows it, and never on a wishlist (a non-student cannot sell), checked in `startConversation` and `makeOffer` (`src/lib/posts/audience.ts`) and shown on the post, message and offer pages. Still not built: ID verification, the one-open-showcase limit for unverified students (needs ID verification to tell them apart), the visitor safety rules, onboarding tour, badges. Keep `ALLOW_VISITORS` off until those exist. Waits for the privacy review, the legal text and the under-18 decision. Review notes and the risks behind each rule are in the conversation of 2026-10-18; the evidence is in `docs/design/principles.md`.

## Decisions (owner)
- Roles: student, faculty or teacher, staff, alumni, visitor. A **school email** means one of the first four, never visitor; a **personal email** means visitor until a school email is added and confirmed by code (decided 2026-10-19, ID check optional after that).
- Sign-up and sign-in role choice (2026-10-24, changed 2026-10-25): sign-up asks for the role first. Student, faculty or teacher, staff and alumni all need a school email; only a Guest may use a personal email (`signUpRoleProblem` in `src/lib/identity/sign-up-rules.ts`). A personal email always makes a Guest account until a school email is confirmed; the role picked preselects the role on the welcome step for someone with a school email. A personal email still needs `ALLOW_VISITORS` (on in staging, off in production). Sign-in asks for a role too, but the saved role wins: a different choice signs in and shows which role applies (`/sign-in?chose=`). Choosing a role never changes permissions.
- Sign-in stays the emailed code. Sending moves to a provider without Gmail's 500 a day cap (Resend or Brevo) **before** visitors are allowed in.
- Unverified students can browse, buy and post wishlists. **An unverified student may have one open showcase listing**; verification lifts the limit.
- Unverified student showcase rule (2026-10-24, in force while `R2_ID_BUCKET` is set): one open, reserved or hidden showcase at a time. The first completed sale of one of their showcases ends the allowance for good: they need a current ID check to post, relist or bulk-relist any more showcases, even if that listing was later deleted (a deleted post keeps its deals, which is what the rule reads, so sales before this rule count too; `src/lib/posts/showcase-limit.ts`). A listing withdrawn, deleted or expired without a completed sale frees the slot. The listing already open and deals in progress carry on. Wishlists are not affected. Reminders: `IdCheckReminder` on the new showcase page and My posts.
- Selling through a Wishlist (2026-10-25): on a Wishlist the one who answers it is the seller, so a completed Wishlist deal counts as the first sale too, and after it a student without a current ID check cannot answer another Wishlist (`supplyBlock` in `src/lib/posts/showcase-limit.ts`, checked in `makeOffer`; the offer page says why and links to the ID check). Deals in progress, listings already open and a student's own Wishlists are not affected. A free handover counts as a sale.
- Reports and hiding (2026-10-25): a listing is hidden when 3 different active members **with a school email** have an open report on it. A Guest's report is stored and the admin sees it, but does not count toward hiding, because a personal email costs nothing. A child safety report still hides on the first report, from anyone.
- Sign-up code budget (2026-10-25): codes for new accounts may use at most half of the day's mail limit (`SIGN_UP_CODES_PER_DAY` in `src/lib/rate-limit.ts`, 48 on the free plan; it follows `MAIL_DAILY_LIMIT`). Past it, sign-up says to try tomorrow; password resets, support messages and codes for existing addresses are not affected. A request for an address that already has an account does not use the budget.
- Faculty, staff, alumni and visitors browse, buy and post wishlists only.
- Each showcase has "allow non-students and visitors to buy" (default off; existing listings stay students-only). Without it the listing cannot be bought or messaged by non-students. Checked at message start, offer and reserve, on every listing.
- Non-students can message only students whose listing allows it. Non-students can redeem perks (**owner to confirm with partners**).
- Bought stays visible only to the student. Sold and granted are public to signed-in people (built 2026-10-18).
- Meet-ups 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM (built 2026-10-18).

## Verification
Built 2026-10-22 (off until `R2_ID_BUCKET` is set; see docs/release.md item 13): consent screen as its own step, ID or registration plus a photo holding it, admin review with every view logged, pictures deleted at the decision, withdrawal in one tap, 7-day cleanup, 12-month validity, 3 rejections then stop, the ID also counts as the age check, one open showcase for members without a check. Built later the same day: a notification when the owner decides, the privacy policy section, and the alumni path (graduates show a diploma, certificate of graduation or alumni ID, never a transcript; `documentKindsFor` in `src/lib/verification/config.ts`).

Upload of ID or COR plus a photo holding it, reviewed by an admin; "verify later" from the profile; status shown in notifications. Built around deletion: private bucket separate from listing photos, admin-only short links, images deleted when a decision is made (keep status, reviewer, date, reason), consent text on the capture screen, limit on resubmissions, expiry after 12 months with re-verification, an alumni path for graduates. Reports and reviews count only from verified students, so sock-puppet visitors cannot hide a listing. The under-18 position must be decided first.

## Onboarding order
Role, then profile (handle, name, short bio), then "verify now or later", then a skippable feature tour.

## Badges
School mark, role, and "ID checked" only when it was. The word "verified" always says what was checked.
