# Child Safety Standards

These are our standards against the sexual abuse and exploitation of children. They say what is not allowed, how to report it, what we do, and who to call. Last updated 2026-10-22. They sit beside the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Community Guidelines.

## In short

- **Zero tolerance.** Anything that sexually abuses, exploits or endangers a child is banned. We remove it and close the account for good.
- **A child is anyone under 18.** It does not matter whether they have an account, or whether they say they agree.
- **Report it.** Use **Report** on a listing or a person and choose "A child is being harmed, or sexual content involving someone under 18". A listing reported this way is hidden right away.
- **Or write to us:** support@studiskarte.com, with the subject **URGENT - CHILD SAFETY**.
- **If a child is in danger now, call 911.** Do not wait for us.

## Why we publish this

Studiskarte Marketplace is for students, and some members may be under 18. We allow members aged 16 and over. A place that may have young members must say clearly what it forbids and how people can reach us. Parents, guardians, teachers, schools and the authorities can all use this page.

The law behind these standards is Republic Act No. 11930, the Anti-Online Sexual Abuse or Exploitation of Children (OSAEC) and Anti-Child Sexual Abuse or Exploitation Materials (CSAEM) Act. We follow it as far as it applies to a platform like ours. Where this page and the Terms differ, the Terms win.

## What is not allowed

None of this is allowed anywhere in the app: in listings, photos, messages, profiles, reviews or reports. It does not matter if it is a joke, "just a story", a drawing, or made by AI.

- **Child sexual abuse or exploitation material (CSAEM).** Any picture, video, audio or writing that shows a child in sexual activity, or shows a child as an object of sex. This includes material that only looks like a child, or says it is one.
- **Sexualising a child.** Describing or showing a child in a sexual way, or in a way meant to excite someone.
- **Grooming and luring.** Befriending or pressuring a child to prepare for sexual abuse, or trying to move a child to another app, or to a meeting, for that reason.
- **Sexual extortion ("sextortion").** Threatening to share someone's private pictures to get more pictures, sex, money or anything else.
- **Asking for sexual pictures or contact** from someone who is, or may be, under 18.
- **Selling, offering, advertising or asking for** any of the above. This includes a listing that says it has such material, whether or not it does.
- **Livestreaming or streaming** the sexual abuse or exploitation of a child.
- **Sharing private pictures of a person without their consent**, whatever their age.

These rules also cover what starts on the app and continues somewhere else. If an account is used to approach a child and harm them elsewhere, we treat it as a breach of these standards.

## What we do to prevent it

We cannot see everything as it happens, and we do not promise to. This is what is in place:

- **Reporting on every listing and person**, with a child safety reason at the top of the list.
- **One report is enough.** An ordinary listing needs three different members to report it before it is hidden. A listing reported for child safety is hidden on the first report.
- **A person reads every child safety report.** They go to the top of the review queue, ahead of everything else, and the owner is alerted by email.
- **Blocking.** Any member can block another. The other person is not told.
- **Only students post showcases.** Faculty, staff, alumni and visitors can only post wishlists, and can only contact a showcase seller if the seller allowed it.
- **We do not sell personal data** and do not show advertising.

## How to report to us

- **In the app.** Open the listing or the person's profile, tap **Report**, and choose the child safety reason. You do not have to be sure, and you do not have to be the person affected. You can block the person at the same time.
- **By email.** Anyone can write to support@studiskarte.com with the subject **URGENT - CHILD SAFETY**: members, parents, guardians, teachers, schools, child protection groups and the authorities. You do not need an account. Please include:
  - the link to the listing, or the person's handle;
  - the date and time you saw it, and your time zone;
  - what you saw, in a few words.

**Do not send, save, screenshot or forward the material itself, not even to us.** Having or sharing it is a crime, even if you mean well. Tell us where it is. We will look at it.

## What we do after a report

- **We act fast.** A clear report about a child is looked at first. We remove clearly illegal content at once and aim to do so within 24 hours of the report, which is also the time the law gives. If a report is unclear, we may ask for more information or take a little longer, and we will not leave content up longer than the law allows.
- **A person decides.** No computer decides alone.
- **We close the account for good.** If a breach is confirmed or looks very likely, the account is closed and cannot come back. This is not the ordinary appeal route.
- **We keep the evidence.** We keep the content and account records needed for the authorities for as long as the law or a lawful order requires. This is so even if someone deletes their account or asks us to erase their data.
- **We report to the authorities.** We tell the proper Philippine authorities, and give them the information the law and their orders require. We do not warn the account holder first, and where the law allows we may not tell them afterwards.
- **We may not tell you the result.** Telling a reporter what we did can harm an investigation or the privacy of a child. Every valid report is acted on.

Removing something, or reporting it, in good faith is protected by the law. If you report in good faith you will not be punished, even if it turns out to be a mistake. Reporting in bad faith, to hurt someone, may lead us to act against you.

## If it happened to you

- **It is not your fault.** If you are under 18 and sent a sexual picture of yourself, or were pressured or tricked into it, you are the victim, not a wrongdoer. We will not blame or shame you, and we will not make your name public.
- **Tell a person you trust:** a parent or guardian, a teacher, a school guidance counsellor.
- **Report to us** (above) and **block** the person. Keep the chat if you can; do not delete it.
- **Do not send more pictures or money,** even if they threaten you. Pay nothing.
- **Call the MAKABATA Helpline, 1383**, open 24 hours a day, for help with child abuse and OSAEC.
- **In an emergency, call 911.**

## Reporting to the authorities directly

You can report to the authorities first, or at the same time as you report to us. Do not wait for us. Under Republic Act No. 11930 and its rules, anyone with direct knowledge of a case may report it, in person, in writing or by phone, to:

- the nearest **police** station, in particular the **PNP Women and Children Protection Center** ("Aleng Pulis": 0966-725-5961 or 0919-777-7377) and the **PNP Women and Children Cybercrime Protection Unit** (02 8723-0401 local 7494, or 0968-896-4759);
- the **NBI Anti-Human Trafficking Division**: (02) 8521-9208;
- the **Department of Justice Office of Cybercrime**: cybercrime@doj.gov.ph, (02) 8524-8216;
- the **NCC-OSAEC-CSAEM Secretariat**: ncc.osaec.csaem@doj.gov.ph, (02) 8524-8939;
- the **1343 Actionline Against Human Trafficking**: 1343, 1343actionline@cmev.gov.ph;
- the **barangay** (its Council for the Protection of Children), the **city or municipal social welfare office**, or your **school's child protection committee**;
- the **MAKABATA Helpline 1383**.

Whoever receives the report must act on it within 24 hours. These numbers were copied from the official NCC-OSAEC-CSAEM reporting page on 2026-10-22; they may change.

## Safety tips for members under 18

- Meet only in a public place with people around, in daylight, and take a friend or an adult. Tell a parent or guardian where you are going.
- Do not share your home address, your ID, your schedule or private pictures with someone you have just met.
- Keep chats in the app. Be careful when someone wants to move to another app.
- Be careful of anyone who flatters you, gives you gifts, asks you to keep secrets or asks for pictures. Leave and report.
- Say no. You can stop, leave or cancel at any time.

## For parents and guardians

If your child uses the app, talk to them about these standards. You can write to us at any time, about a concern, a report, or to ask us to close their account: support@studiskarte.com. Please say who you are and which account you mean.

## Who to contact

- **Email:** support@studiskarte.com, subject **URGENT - CHILD SAFETY**.
- **Response:** child safety reports are read first, and we aim to act within 24 hours. Properly identified requests from the authorities about a child in danger are answered as soon as we can.

The owner runs the app alone. For other matters (ordinary reports, data requests) use the same address.

## Changes

We review these standards at least once a year, and whenever the app or the law changes. We will change the date at the top when we do.
