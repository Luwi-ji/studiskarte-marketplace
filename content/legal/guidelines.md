# Community Guidelines

These guidelines say what is welcome in Studiskarte Marketplace. They sit beside the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Last updated 2026-10-21.

## Be a good neighbour
- Be honest. Use real photos of the real item. Say what is wrong with it.
- Be kind. No harassment, bullying, threats, hate, or sexual content.
- Be reliable. Answer messages, keep your appointments, and cancel early if plans change.
- Keep it on the app. Say what you want in the listing, and do not pressure anyone to pay before you meet.

## What you may not post
- Past exams, answer keys, finished graded work, or any help meant for cheating.
- School IDs, accounts, logins, or anyone else's personal information.
- Medicine, drugs, alcohol, tobacco, vapes, weapons, or anything illegal to sell.
- Fake or stolen goods, or things that are not yours to sell.
- Scams, misleading offers, or anything that asks for money in a way the listing does not say.

## Food, housing and services
- If you sell food, follow the rules that apply, and list what is in it.
- If you offer housing, you are responsible for being allowed to offer it and for what you say about it.
- **The app never holds money, so paying for a service has no protection.** Agree what you will do and what it costs before you start. Be careful paying in advance to someone you do not know.

## Meeting safely
- Meet in a public place, in daylight where you can.
- Tell a friend where you are going and who you are meeting.
- You can say no, leave, or cancel at any time.

## Reports and blocking
- Use **Report** on a listing or a person when something breaks these guidelines.
- Use **Block** to stop someone from contacting you.
- A listing reported by three different members is hidden at once and reviewed by the owner. The owner can restore it, remove it, or suspend an account.
- False or repeated reports used to harm someone may lead to action against the reporter.

## What happens if the guidelines are broken
A listing may be hidden or removed. An account may be suspended. We do not suspend accounts automatically.

Questions or something urgent: support@studiskarte.com.
