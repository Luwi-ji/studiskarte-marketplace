# Privacy Policy

Studiskarte Marketplace ("the app", "we") is a marketplace where students and other members of the school community in the Philippines offer things and services and look for things they want. This policy explains what personal data the app collects, why, who else handles it, how long it is kept, and what you can do about it. Last updated 2026-10-19.

The app is run by its owner, an individual, not a company. You can reach us at studiskartemarketplace@gmail.com.

## What we collect

When you create an account:
- **Your email address.** A school email address (ending in .edu.ph) or another email address. We send a 6-digit code to it to prove it is yours.
- **Your password,** stored only as a salted hash. We cannot read it.
- **If you add a school email later,** that school email and the school it belongs to, and the role you choose (student, faculty or teacher, staff, alumni).
- **If you use Google to sign in,** we receive the email address Google confirms for you. We do not keep your Google name or picture.
- **Your handle and display name,** which other signed-in people see, and an optional profile picture.

When you use the app:
- **Listings:** the title, description, price, category, photos, availability and where you meet. Photos have their location and camera data removed when they are uploaded.
- **Messages and pictures** you send to another member, offers, reservations, handover times and places, and the reviews you give and receive.
- **Activity:** who you follow, listings you heart (hearts are private), people you block, reports you make, and views of listings (counted once per person per day, shown only to the listing's owner as a number).
- **Device and session data:** when you sign in we store a session with your IP address and browser details, for 30 days or until you sign out.
- **Device alerts,** if you switch them on: an address your phone's push service gives us. Alerts show a headline and a listing title, never message text.
- **Calendar link,** if you make one: only a fingerprint (hash) of the private link is stored.
- **Which version of these terms you accepted, and when.**

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers. The only cookie is the one that keeps you signed in.

## Why we use it

- To create your account, sign you in, and keep your account safe (codes, passwords, sign-in limits).
- To run the marketplace: show listings, let members message each other, make offers and reservations, arrange a handover, and show reviews.
- To send you the mail and alerts the app needs: sign-in codes, password reset codes, and notices about your listings and reservations.
- To keep the community safe: reports, blocks, limits on spam, and review by the owner of reported listings and people.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising. We never handle payments: money changes hands between members outside the app.

## What others can see

Signed-in members can see your handle, display name, picture, school (shown as the school name, or "Visitor" if you have none), the number of followers you have, your listings, your reviews and ratings, and how many items you sold or gave away. Your email address, school email and password are never shown to other members. Messages are visible only to the two people in the conversation (and to the owner if one of you reports it).

## Who else handles your data

We use these services to run the app. Each receives only what it needs:
- **Vercel** hosts the app and sees requests to it (including IP addresses) in its logs for a short time.
- **Neon** hosts the database (in the United States).
- **Cloudflare R2** stores listing photos, message pictures and profile pictures. They can only be reached through short-lived links that the app creates for signed-in members.
- **Google (Gmail)** sends our emails, so Google sees the address we send a code to. **Google sign-in** is used only if you choose it.
- **Push services** run by your phone's maker or browser (for example Apple, Google or Mozilla) carry device alerts.

Photos, messages and reservations are stored in these services, some outside the Philippines.

## How long we keep it

- Sign-in codes: 5 to 10 minutes. Sessions: 30 days. Sign-in attempt counters: removed daily.
- Your account data: until you delete your account.
- Listings that you withdraw or that expire disappear from your lists after a day or a week, but are kept in our database together with their photos, offers and reservations, so the history and reviews of other members stay correct.
- Messages, finished reservations and reviews stay after you delete your account, shown under the name "Deleted account".

## Deleting your account and your other rights

In **Settings, Delete my account** you can delete your account at any time. This erases your email and school email, handle, display name, picture, password, sessions, device alerts, notifications, follows, hearts, blocks and view history, withdraws your open listings, and ends reservations still being arranged. Messages, finished reservations, reviews and the photos of your listings that others' history depends on stay, without your name. If you want any of that removed too, write to us.

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) you have the right to be informed, to access your data, to correct it, to object to its use, to have it erased or blocked, to data portability, and to claim compensation for damage caused by misuse of it. To use any of these, write to studiskartemarketplace@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

## Young people

The app is meant for people in higher education. If you are under 18, use it only with the permission of a parent or guardian.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will change the date above and ask you to accept the new version the next time you use the app.
