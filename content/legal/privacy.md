# Privacy Policy

Studiskarte Marketplace ("the app", "we") is a marketplace where students and other members of the school community in the Philippines offer things and services and look for things they want. This policy explains what personal data the app collects, why, who else handles it, how long it is kept, and what you can do about it. Last updated 2026-10-25.

The app is run by its owner, an individual, not a company. You can reach us at support@studiskarte.com.

## What we collect

When you create an account:
- **Your email address.** A school email address (ending in .edu.ph) or another email address. We send a 6-digit code to it to prove it is yours.
- **Your password,** stored only as a salted hash. We cannot read it.
- **If you add a school email later,** that school email and the school it belongs to, and the role you choose (student, faculty or teacher, staff, alumni).
- **If you use Google to sign in,** Google sends us your basic profile (the email address it confirms for you, your name and your picture). We keep only the email address and discard the name and picture. We do not ask for or read anything else in your Google account.
- **Your handle and display name,** which other signed-in people see, and an optional profile picture.

When you use the app:
- **Listings:** the title, description, price, category, photos, availability and where you meet. Photos have their location and camera data removed when they are uploaded.
- **Messages and pictures** you send to another member, offers, reservations, handover times and places, and the reviews you give and receive.
- **Activity:** who you follow, listings you heart (hearts are private), people you block, reports you make, and views of listings (counted once per person per day, shown only to the listing's owner as a number).
- **Device and session data:** when you sign in we store a session with your IP address and browser details, for 30 days or until you sign out.
- **Device alerts,** if you switch them on: an address your phone's push service gives us. Alerts show a headline and a listing title, never message text.
- **Calendar link,** if you make one: only a fingerprint (hash) of the private link is stored.
- **Profile details you choose to add:** a short bio and where you are based (for example "Sampaloc, Manila"). Other signed-in members see them on your profile.
- **Perks:** when you claim a perk, the perk, the shop, your voucher code, and when you claimed and redeemed it. A shop sees only counts, voucher codes and times, never your name or handle. We also count the wrong shop PINs typed on your phone, for a short time, to block guessing.
- **How you found us and your first steps:** if you accepted the "Measuring visits" choice before signing up, the campaign tag of the link you used (for example "instagram") is saved on your account. We also note, without the content, that you made your first listing, sent your first message or confirmed your first handover, to learn where people get stuck.
- **Founding User badge and tour:** whether you were one of the first members to join (the badge), and whether you have seen the first-time tour.
- **Which version of these terms you accepted, and when.**

We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics. The cookie that keeps you signed in is the only one the app needs. A second, optional cookie exists only if you accept it on the landing page (see the next section).

## If you only visit the landing page

You do not need an account to read the landing page. What it can keep, and when:
- **Essential storage:** the cookie that keeps you signed in after you log in, and your light or dark mode, stored in your browser. Always on.
- **Measuring visits, only if you accept.** We count your visit without your name or email, and, if the link you used was tagged (for example from Instagram), keep that tag in a cookie for 7 days so we know which post brought you. Your choice is saved in your browser, and you can change it any time with "Cookie settings" in the page footer. If you reject, nothing is counted and an earlier tag cookie is removed.
- **Message us:** the name (optional), email address and message you type. We receive it as an email at support@studiskarte.com and it also waits in our private inbox so it is not lost. We keep it until we have answered it, then delete it.
- **Perk votes and shop suggestions:** the kind of perk you vote for, or the shop name you type, with the date. No name, email or account is attached. Your IP address is used only for a short-lived limit against spam, and is not saved with the vote.

## Why we use it

- To create your account, sign you in, and keep your account safe (codes, passwords, sign-in limits).
- To run the marketplace: show listings, let members message each other, make offers and reservations, arrange a handover, and show reviews.
- To send you the mail and alerts the app needs: sign-in codes, password reset codes, and notices about your listings and reservations.
- To keep the community safe: reports, blocks, limits on spam, and review by the owner of reported listings and people.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising. We never handle payments: money changes hands between members outside the app.

## What others can see

Signed-in members can see your handle, display name, picture, school (shown as the school name, or "Visitor" if you have none), the number of followers you have, your listings, your reviews and ratings, and how many items you sold or gave away. Your email address, school email and password are never shown to other members. Messages are visible only to the two people in the conversation (and to the owner if one of you reports it).

## Who else handles your data

We use these services to run the app. Each receives only what it needs:
- **Vercel** hosts the app and sees requests to it (including IP addresses) in its logs for a short time.
- **Neon** hosts the database (in the United States).
- **Cloudflare** stores listing photos, message pictures and profile pictures (R2), stores encrypted database backups in a separate private R2 area for up to 30 days, manages our domain name, and forwards the mail you send to our support address. Photos can only be reached through short-lived links that the app creates for signed-in members. If you use the ID check, Cloudflare also stores those pictures in a separate private area that only the owner can open. On sign-up, password reset and the public contact form, Cloudflare Turnstile receives technical request information such as your IP address and browser details to distinguish people from automated abuse.
- **Resend** sends our emails (sign-in, sign-up, password reset and school email codes), so Resend sees the address we send a code to and the code itself. **Google sign-in** is used only if you choose it, and then Google sees that you sign in to the app.
- **Sentry** receives error reports when something breaks in the app, so we can fix it. We configure it to leave out your name, email address, IP address, cookies and what you typed. A report can still show which page failed. Sentry stores data outside the Philippines.
- **Push services** run by your phone's maker or browser (for example Apple, Google or Mozilla) carry device alerts.

Photos, messages and reservations are stored in these services, some outside the Philippines.

## ID check

The ID check is optional. It lets you show that you are a real person who studies or works at the school you chose, and that you meet the minimum age of 16, so other members can trust a badge that says "ID checked". Without an ID check you can still browse, message, make offers, reserve, and post Wishlist items. You can have one open Showcase listing at a time until your ID is checked.

- **What we collect.** A picture of your school ID or certificate of registration, and a photo of you holding it. If you are a graduate, you show proof that you graduated instead: a diploma, a certificate of graduation or an alumni ID, but not a transcript of grades. These show information about your education and may show your age, so we treat them with extra care. You may cover your ID number.
- **Why.** Only to decide whether to give your account an "ID checked" badge: to check who you are, your role at the school you chose, and that you meet the minimum age. We do not use the pictures for anything else.
- **Your consent.** We ask for it on its own screen, before you can upload anything, separate from accepting these terms. The screen says what we ask for, why, who sees it, what happens next and how to withdraw. You can say no and use the rest of the app. We keep a record that you agreed: which version of the screen you saw, when, and, if you withdraw, when.
- **Who sees it.** Only the owner. The pictures are kept in a private storage area, separate from listing photos, and the owner opens them only through links that stop working after a few minutes. Each time a picture is opened it is written down (who, which check, when). We do not share them with anyone, and they do not appear anywhere in the app.
- **After the decision.** The pictures are deleted from storage first, and only then is the decision saved. We keep the result (approved or rejected), who decided, the date, and a short reason. If we reject, you will see the reason. We do not copy details from your ID into our records.
- **If you start but do not finish,** or the check waits more than 7 days, the pictures are deleted and the check is closed.
- **If we reject it,** you may try again, up to 3 times in total.
- **How long a check lasts.** 12 months. After that the badge ends and you can ask again.
- **Withdrawing your consent.** You can withdraw at any time in Settings, "ID check", with one tap. If the pictures are still waiting, we delete them right away and stop the check. If we have already decided, the pictures are already gone. Your badge is removed. You can check again later. Withdrawing does not undo what we did while you agreed. We keep the record of the check result and of your consent for 3 years after you delete your account, so that we can show what you agreed to.
- **If you delete your account,** any pictures still waiting are deleted.

## How long we keep it

- Sign-in codes: 5 to 10 minutes. Sessions: 30 days. Sign-in attempt counters: removed daily.
- Your account data: until you delete your account.
- Voucher history: kept with your anonymised account after you delete it, so a shop's totals stay correct. It no longer shows your name or handle.
- Listings that you withdraw or that expire disappear from your lists after a day or a week, but are kept in our database together with their photos, offers and reservations, so the history and reviews of other members stay correct.
- Messages, finished reservations and reviews stay after you delete your account, shown under the name "Deleted account".

## Deleting your account and your other rights

In **Settings, Delete my account** you can delete your account at any time. This erases your email and school email, handle, display name, picture, password, sessions, device alerts, notifications, follows, hearts, blocks and view history, withdraws your open listings, and ends reservations still being arranged. Messages, finished reservations, reviews and the photos of your listings that others' history depends on stay, without your name. If you want any of that removed too, write to us.

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) you have the right to be informed, to access your data, to correct it, to object to its use, to have it erased or blocked, to data portability, and to claim compensation for damage caused by misuse of it. To use any of these, write to support@studiskarte.com. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

## Young people

You must be 16 or older to create an account, and we ask you to confirm it when you sign up. If you are younger, please do not sign up. If we find out that someone under 16 has an account, we may close it.

Members aged 16 and 17 are welcome. If you are under 18, tell a parent or guardian that you use the app. Your listings, messages and profile are handled the same way as everyone else's, and you can use Report and Block at any time. Our Child Safety Standards say how we protect members under 18 and how to report a problem.

If we have to keep information to help protect a child, for example because the authorities ask us to, we keep it for as long as the law requires, even if the account is deleted. We may give it to the authorities, and we may not tell the account holder.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will change the date above and ask you to accept the new version the next time you use the app.
